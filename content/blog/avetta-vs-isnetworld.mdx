# Avetta vs ISNetworld: Key Differences Contractors Need to Know

As a contractor, you may need to be qualified in both Avetta and ISNetworld. While these platforms serve similar purposes - prequalifying contractors for safety and compliance - they have important differences that affect how you manage your accounts.

## Platform Overview

### ISNetworld
- **Focus**: Oil & gas, utilities, construction
- **Grading**: A+ to F letter grades
- **Primary Review**: RAVS (Review and Verification Services)
- **Strength**: Detailed safety program review

### Avetta
- **Focus**: Broader industry coverage
- **Grading**: Pass/fail with compliance percentage
- **Primary Review**: Prequalification questionnaire
- **Strength**: Insurance and financial verification

## Key Differences

### 1. Qualification Process

**ISNetworld**
- Extensive safety program review
- Detailed questionnaires (RAVS, MS&Q)
- Grade-based system
- Annual RAVS renewal

**Avetta**
- Prequalification questionnaire
- Insurance verification emphasis
- Pass/fail system
- Rolling updates as documents expire

### 2. Documentation Requirements

**ISNetworld Requires:**
- Comprehensive written safety program
- 3 years of OSHA 300 logs
- Detailed training records
- Management commitment documentation

**Avetta Requires:**
- Basic safety programs
- Current insurance certificates
- W-9 and business license
- EMR (Experience Modification Rate)

### 3. Client Connections

**ISNetworld**
- Client-specific requirements added after connection
- Client can view your grade before connecting
- RAVS grade applies across all clients

**Avetta**
- Client-specific requirements during initial setup
- Each client may have unique questionnaires
- Overall compliance score visible to clients

### 4. Cost Structure

**ISNetworld**
- Subscription fee (tier-based on company size)
- RAVS review fee
- Additional fees for rush reviews

**Avetta**
- Subscription fee
- Per-client connection fees possible
- Premium support options

## Which Platform is More Important?

The answer depends on your industry and target clients:

**Choose ISNetworld if you work in:**
- Oil and gas
- Utilities
- Heavy construction
- Pipeline work

**Choose Avetta if you work in:**
- Manufacturing facilities
- Retail/commercial construction
- Mining
- Multiple industries

**Reality Check**: Most contractors need both platforms to maximize contract opportunities.

## Managing Both Platforms Efficiently

### Document Reuse
Many documents work for both platforms:
- Safety manuals
- Insurance certificates
- Training records
- OSHA logs

### Streamlined Approach
1. Create comprehensive documentation that exceeds both platforms' requirements
2. Maintain a centralized document library
3. Schedule updates on a quarterly basis
4. Track expirations across both platforms

### Common Pitfalls
- Updating one platform but forgetting the other
- Different safety manual versions on each platform
- Expired documents on one platform while current on the other

## Best Practices for Dual Management

1. **Use Consistent Documentation**
   - Same safety manual for both (if it meets all requirements)
   - Unified training program
   - Single source for OSHA logs

2. **Coordinate Updates**
   - Update both platforms simultaneously
   - Use a shared calendar for expirations
   - Review both accounts monthly

3. **Understand Unique Requirements**
   - Know which clients use which platform
   - Understand platform-specific questionnaires
   - Maintain grade in ISNetworld while meeting Avetta compliance

4. **Consider Professional Management**
   - Managing both platforms is time-consuming
   - Specialists can optimize both accounts simultaneously
   - Reduce risk of missing requirements

## Time Investment Comparison

- Ongoing maintenance: 2-3 hours/month

**Avetta**:
- Initial setup: 8-12 hours
- Annual renewal: 4-6 hours
- Ongoing maintenance: 2-3 hours/month

**Managing Both**:
