# How to Improve Your ISNetworld Grade from C to A+

Achieving an A+ grade in ISNetworld is crucial for contractors who want to work with major clients. If you're currently sitting at a C or lower grade, you might be losing out on valuable contracts. The good news? With the right approach, you can significantly improve your grade in 30-60 days.

## Understanding ISNetworld Grades

ISNetworld grades your account based on several factors:

- **RAVS Review**: Your safety program documentation
- **MS&Q**: Management Systems & Questionnaires
- **Client-Specific Requirements**: Individual client needs
- **Documentation Quality**: Completeness and compliance of uploaded documents

## Step 1: Complete Your RAVS Questionnaire

The RAVS (Review and Verification Services) questionnaire is the foundation of your ISNetworld grade. Here's how to excel:

### Required Documentation

- Written safety program
- OSHA 300 logs (3 years)
- Insurance certificates
- Training records
- Drug and alcohol policy
- Emergency action plan

### Best Practices

1. **Be Thorough**: Don't skip any questions
2. **Use Specific Examples**: Reference actual programs and procedures
3. **Update Regularly**: Keep your answers current
4. **Upload Quality Documents**: Clear, professional documentation matters

## Step 2: Optimize Your MS&Q

The Management Systems & Questionnaires section evaluates your overall safety management approach.

### Key Areas to Address

- **Management Commitment**: Demonstrate leadership involvement
- **Safety Training**: Document all training programs
- **Incident Investigation**: Show your process for investigating accidents
- **Subcontractor Management**: Explain how you vet and manage subs

## Step 3: Upload Complete Documentation

Missing or outdated documents are grade killers. Ensure you have:

- Current insurance (updated annually)
- Recent OSHA logs
- Safety manual (updated within 12 months)
- Training certificates
- Client-specific documentation

## Step 4: Address Client Requirements

Different clients have different requirements within ISNetworld. Make sure you:

- Review each client's specific needs
- Complete client-specific questionnaires
- Upload client-requested documents
- Respond promptly to client inquiries

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Incomplete Questionnaires**: Skipping questions lowers your grade
2. **Outdated Documents**: Expired insurance or old OSHA logs hurt you
3. **Generic Safety Programs**: Your manual should be specific to your operations
4. **Ignoring Deadlines**: Late responses to client requests damage your reputation

## Timeline for Improvement

- **Week 1-2**: Complete all questionnaires
- **Week 3-4**: Upload all required documentation
- **Week 5-6**: Address any deficiencies noted by ISNetworld
- **Week 7-8**: Review and final optimization

Most companies see grade improvement within 30-60 days of implementing these changes.
